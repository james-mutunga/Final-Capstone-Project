** *Physical Infrastructure and Retail Layout Analytics* **

When businesses open a store or commercial venue, they make major physical layout decisions—such as how tall the ceilings are, how wide the aisles are, where product displays are positioned, and where the checkout counters are placed.

However, business owners and store managers often don't know how these physical space choices actually affect their customers. Does an open, high-ceiling layout encourage people to linger and spend more? Do narrow aisles or poor counter placements cause foot-traffic bottlenecks that make customers rush out or leave empty-handed?

Without data, companies design their physical spaces based on guesswork. This project connects **physical layout design** with **customer movement and sales data** so businesses can build spaces that feel great to shop in and generate higher revenue.
